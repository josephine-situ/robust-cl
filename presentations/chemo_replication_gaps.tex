\begin{frame}{What caused the earlier ``gaps''}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Stale ground-truth (GT) evaluation ensemble (primary).} Table 6
      metrics are scored by a 6-model GT ensemble. Earlier runs fit it on
      \emph{raw} toxicity targets, so the \texttt{LinearSVR}/\texttt{XGBoost}
      members scored far below paper Table EC.11 (e.g.\ OS SVM $R^2{=}0.01$ vs.\
      $0.47$), shifting predicted toxicity percentiles across the $0.6$ threshold
      and depressing OS.
    \item \textbf{Fix (commit ``Align models''):} fit GT on \emph{percentile-scaled}
      targets with the paper's fixed hyperparameters. Not a library change ---
      environment stays \texttt{scikit-learn 1.8} / \texttt{xgboost 3.2}. GT now
      reproduces EC.11 (mean $|\Delta R^2|{=}0.008$).
    \item \textbf{Cohort handling already correct:} Table 6 uses the samestore
      cohort (patients feasible across constraint modes) with one shared
      \texttt{given} baseline, via \texttt{samestore\_eval\_mask}.
    \item Data pipeline is faithful: train/test $=320/96$ and given satisfaction
      match the paper to $<1$\,pp on full and samestore cohorts.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Methods Compared}
  \footnotesize
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
  \begin{tabular}{p{2.3cm}p{7.7cm}}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} \\
      \midrule
      \textbf{Nominal} &
          Single model on observed labels;
          no robustness \\
      \textbf{Robust Param} &
          Single model; embed with split-threshold
          robustness ($\rho{=}0.05$) \\
      \textbf{Robust Cls.} &
          $P{=}25$ shared bootstrap resamples;
          embed model with lowest worst-case OOB error \\
      \textbf{Wrapper} &
          $P{=}25$ bootstrap estimators; chance constraint
          allowing $\alpha{=}10\%$ violation \\
      \textbf{Tree Violation} &
          Paper baseline: per-tree RF chance constraint
          ($\alpha{=}25\%$ trees may violate) \\
      \textbf{CP} &
          Iterative cutting planes;
          localized bootstrap separation at $x^k$ \\
      \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  
  \medskip
  \normalsize
  \textbf{Uncertainty calibration:}
  resample observed training labels (no parametric $\delta$);
  wrapper and robust cls.\ share fixed $P$ draws;
  CP resamples $k$-nearest arms ($k{=}10\%$ of $n_{\mathrm{train}}$),
  $20$ candidates per separation call
  
\end{frame}
