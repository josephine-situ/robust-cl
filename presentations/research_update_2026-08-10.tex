\begin{frame}{1. One $\mathcal{D}$, three methods}
  \small
  Was: each method had its own uncertainty notion. Method differences were partly
  input differences.
  \[
    \mathcal D_c = \{\delta: |\delta_i|\le\varepsilon_c,\ \lVert\delta\rVert_1 \le
      \texttt{budget\_frac}\cdot n\,\varepsilon_c\},
    \qquad \varepsilon_c = \varepsilon_0\cdot\mathrm{scale}(y_c)
  \]
  \begin{center}\small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
    \toprule
    Method & Use of $\mathcal D$ & Dial \\
    \midrule
    Robust Reg. & robustifies the \emph{fit}       & \texttt{label\_eps} \\
    Wrapper     & chance-constrains a sample       & $\alpha$ \\
    CP (ours)   & separates \emph{lazily}          & $\tau$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
  \medskip
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Draws are \textbf{vertices} --- where Robust Reg.'s inner max sits. No
      method gets a weaker adversary.
    \item \textbf{Coherence}: one flag, crossed with all three.
    \item $\varepsilon_0$, \texttt{budget\_frac}: shared constants, not knobs.
    \item Settles old open question 1: assumed noise, not bootstrap.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{2. Radius = unexplained spread, not marginal}
  \small
  $\delta$ perturbs labels \emph{before} retraining $\Rightarrow$ radius lives in
  label space. Both candidates do; they measure different things.
  \begin{center}\small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
    \toprule
    & $\mathrm{sd}(y)$ & OOF residual sd & unexplained \\
    \midrule
    Gastric toxicities & 0.288 & 0.249--0.293 & 0.78--1.02 \\
    Synthetic ($\sigma_{\text{true}}=0.100$) & 0.545 & \textbf{0.128} & 0.235 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
  \medskip
  \begin{itemize}
    \item $\mathrm{sd}(y)$ is mostly \emph{signal}. At $\varepsilon_0=1$ it corrupts
      synthetic $4\times$ too hard --- CP would not converge.
    \item Now $\varepsilon_0=1$ = \textbf{one unexplained sd}. Synthetic: $0.128$ vs
      true noise $0.100$, without being told the DGP.
    \item Validation of the estimator, never an input to it.
    \item Caveat: conflates noise with misspecification. Gastric fits are poor, so
      $\mathcal D$ stays wide there. \textbf{No coverage claim.}
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{3. CP was not converging on gastric --- 5 faults}
  \small
  Symptom: hit \texttt{max\_iterations} every run. Every $\tau\lesssim0.5$ gave an
  identical run --- the knob was inert.
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Resampled each iteration}, but $d_0$ frozen at iteration 0. Stopping
      rule compared different samples. Bank now drawn once.
    \item \textbf{MIP gap hard-coded at 1\%} --- the biggest. Gastric objective
      ${\approx}10$, so gap ${\approx}0.1$; separated distances ${\approx}0.007$.
      Cuts below the solver's tolerance $\Rightarrow$ same incumbent. Now $10^{-4}$.
    \item \textbf{Cut removal $\Rightarrow$ cycles.} Nothing is removed now; eligible
      set strictly shrinks; terminates in $\le B$.
    \item \textbf{Drifting coverage baseline} (a ratchet). Now pinned to the nominal
      fit's servable anchors (8/10), set-wise.
    \item \textbf{No rejection memory} --- rollbacks grew $8\to44$/iter. Caching is
      exact once (4) holds. 113/200 draws rejected.
  \end{enumerate}
  \medskip
  \footnotesize
  Faults 1--2 are regime-specific: synthetic never hit the gap (objective
  ${\approx}1.2$, distances ${\approx}0.1$).
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{4. Result: gastric converges}
  \small
  Worst distance by iteration, $\tau=0.1$:
  \[
    0.0101 \;\; 0.0124 \;\; 0.0098 \;\; 0.0055 \;\cdots\; 0.0010 \;\; 0.00074 \;\;
    \mathbf{0.00038}
  \]
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{status=optimal}, \textbf{19 iterations}. Reached the tolerance
      ($0.00048$); did not exhaust iterations.
    \item 0 cut-set repeats, 0 objective regressions. Was: exact period-4 cycle.
    \item Final master: 26 cuts, 9{,}095 vars / 22{,}957 constrs, $B=200$.
    \item Anchor infeasibility flat at the 8/10 baseline --- protected set never
      breached.
    \item Synthetic control unchanged: 6 iterations, objective $-1.23806$.
  \end{itemize}
  \medskip
  \footnotesize
  New diagnostics: cycle status + period, objective regressions, permanent
  rejections, bound-blocked rate. Reported, never auto-acted on.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{5. Two structural results}
  \small
  \textbf{CP $\equiv$ lazy wrapper at $\alpha=0$}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Draw $b$ = pure function of $(\texttt{seed},b)$ $\Rightarrow$ wrapper's $P$
      models are a \emph{prefix} of CP's bank.
    \item At $B=P$: CP ($\tau\to0$) and wrapper ($\alpha=0$) agree,
      $\max|\Delta x| = 0$.
  \end{itemize}
  \medskip
  \textbf{$10\times$ the adversaries, $1/5$ the MIP}
  \begin{center}\small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lrrr@{}}
    \toprule
    & adversaries & vars & constrs \\
    \midrule
    Wrapper & $P=20$  & 31{,}917 & 101{,}172 \\
    CP      & $B=200$ & \textbf{6{,}380} & \textbf{20{,}236} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
  \medskip
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Wrapper embeds all $P$; CP embeds one cut per iteration. The bank is a
      separation \emph{pool}.
    \item $d_0$ is now a 0.9 quantile, not the max --- the max grows with $B$, and
      CP/wrapper run at different $B$ by design.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{6. Evaluation and housekeeping}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Synthetic draws were fake.} No seed was passed, so every
      ``realization'' rebuilt the same dataset --- spread across draws was
      identically \textbf{zero}. Now seeded, at CV-calibrated knobs.
    \item \textbf{10 draws everywhere} (was 30 headline / 10 wrapper). That gap is why
      the wrapper was never comparable and got dropped from the figures.
    \item \textbf{Coherence crossed}, $\theta^\ast$ calibrated per cell --- coherent is
      the stronger adversary, so one shared $\theta^\ast$ would confound coherence with
      conservatism. Coherent Robust Reg.\ implemented.
    \item \textbf{Wrapper restored} to gastric; OS robustification off by default.
    \item \textbf{DLT-only dropped.} Retires a bug: \texttt{\_feas} read the
      all-constraints row regardless of mode.
    \item Synthetic figures now show a min--max band and the \textbf{worst-case edge}.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{7. In flight: cut the whole scenario}
  \small
  Today CP cuts only the constraints breached at the current $x^\ast$. But a scenario
  is one coherent relabeling --- if plausible, \emph{all} its constraints should hold.
  \medskip

  \texttt{cp.cut\_whole\_scenario: true} --- uncommitted, \textbf{not yet run}:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Extends the wrapper equivalence to multi-constraint problems. The wrapper
      already gates one indicator on all constraints.
    \item Makes per-scenario exclusion sound. A partially-cut scenario can breach a
      different constraint at a moved $x^\ast$ and never be re-separated.
    \item Then the stopping rule covers the whole bank, not the scannable remainder.
    \item Cost: ${\approx}5$ embedded models per accepted scenario, was ${\approx}1.4$.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
