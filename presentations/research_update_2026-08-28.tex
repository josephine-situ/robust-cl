\begin{frame}{Gastric: tuned dial, then tested}
    \footnotesize
    Dial$^\ast$ = best objective among cells with feas $\ge 0.9$, solved $\ge 0.5$
    (tuning judge = train-only ensemble). Test judge = full-416 GT ensemble.
    \vspace{0.5em}
    \begin{tabular}{llccc|cc}
        \toprule
        & & \multicolumn{3}{c|}{tuned} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{tested (folds)} \\
        method & dial$^\ast$ & feas & obj & solved & feas & obj \\
        \midrule
        nominal              & --             & 0.891 & \textbf{11.30} & 0.951 & 0.934 & \textbf{11.29} \\
        RHS margin           & $m=0.2$        & 0.931 & 10.97 & 0.922 & 0.951 & 11.01 \\
        wrapper $\rho{=}1$   & $\alpha=0.4$   & 0.930 & 10.89 & 0.592 & 0.970 & 10.94 \\
        wrapper $\rho{=}0.5$ & $\alpha=0.2$   & 0.941 & 10.88 & 0.679 & 0.961 & 10.97 \\
        CP $\rho{=}0.5$      & $\tau=0.03$    & 0.974 & 10.78 & 0.821 & \textbf{0.987} & 10.86 \\
        CP $\rho{=}1$        & $\tau=0.1$     & 0.938 & 9.84  & 0.870 & 0.947 & 10.07 \\
        C-MICL               & \multicolumn{6}{l}{no cell cleared solved $\ge 0.5$} \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \vspace{0.5em}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Under the test judge nominal already clears 0.9} (0.934).
              The tuning proxy is more pessimistic than the evaluation oracle.
        \item So the $0.9$ rule that fixed every dial$^\ast$ is not binding at test.
        \item Objectives are conditional on each cell's own solved contexts:
              wrapper's $10.94$ is on a $59\%$ cohort.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What the mean hides: worst case over training draws}
    \footnotesize
    Outer $m$-out-of-$n$ subsampling, \texttt{all\_constraints} feasibility,
    30 realizations. \textbf{2026-08-13, \texttt{box\_l1} --- stale, shown for shape only.}
    \vspace{0.5em}
    \begin{tabular}{lcccc}
        \toprule
        method & mean & sd & worst case & 10th pct \\
        \midrule
        nominal    & 0.898 & 0.157 & \textbf{0.220} & 0.771 \\
        robust\_reg & 0.923 & 0.075 & 0.684 & 0.791 \\
        CP         & 0.919 & 0.099 & 0.607 & \textbf{0.867} \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \vspace{0.5em}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Means differ by $0.025$; \textbf{worst cases by $3\times$}.
        \item The dial sweep does \textbf{not} compute this --- it has no outer
              resampling loop at all. Only \texttt{run\_chemo\_robust.py} does.
        \item Closest current proxy: worst of 4 temporal test folds ---
              CP $\rho{=}0.5$ \textbf{0.947}, margin $0.923$, wrapper $\rho{=}0.5$ $0.880$,
              nominal $0.900$.
        \item That is a worst \emph{year}, not a worst \emph{draw}. Different quantity.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
